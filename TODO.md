## Small words (same style as se / por / ya / me)

- [x] ⭐ **para**: the natural partner to *por*, and the quiz could finally test *por* vs *para* properly
  - Purpose: *Estudio **para** aprender.*: I study (in order) to learn.
  - Destination: *Salgo **para** Madrid.*: I'm leaving for Madrid.
  - Deadline: *Lo necesito **para** el lunes.*: I need it by Monday.
  - Recipient: *Este regalo es **para** ti.*: This gift is for you.
  - Opinion: ***Para** mí, es demasiado caro.*: For me, it's too expensive.

- [x] ⭐ **lo**: "it/him", but also turns adjectives into ideas
  - Direct object: ***Lo** veo.*: I see him / it.
  - *lo* + adjective = "the … thing": ***Lo** bueno es que…*: The good thing is that…
  - *lo que* = "what": *No entiendo **lo** que dices.*: I don't understand what you're saying.
  - *lo* + adj + *que* = "how …": *No sabes **lo** difícil que es.*: You don't know how hard it is.

- [x] ⭐ **le**: indirect object, plus Spain-specific quirks
  - "to/for him/her/you": ***Le** di el libro.*: I gave him/her the book.
  - With *gustar*-type verbs: *A Ana **le** encanta bailar.*: Ana loves dancing.
  - *le* → *se* before lo/la: *Se **lo** di.* (not *le lo*): I gave it to him.
  - Leísmo (Spain): ***Le** vi ayer.*: I saw him yesterday (instead of *lo vi*).

- [x] ⭐ **que / qué**: probably the most-used word in Spanish
  - "that / which": *El libro **que** compré.*: The book (that) I bought.
  - "than": *Es más alto **que** yo.*: He's taller than me.
  - Wishes: *¡**Que** te mejores!*: Get well soon!
  - Colloquial "because": *Abrígate, **que** hace frío.*: Wrap up, it's cold.
  - *qué* = "what / how": *¡**Qué** bonito!*: How beautiful!

- [x] **como / cómo**
  - "like / as": *Habla **como** su madre.*: She talks like her mother.
  - "since / because" (at the start of a sentence): ***Como** llovía, no salimos.*: Since it was raining, we didn't go out.
  - "about / roughly": *Había **como** diez personas.*: There were about ten people.
  - Threat/warning "if": ***Como** no vengas, me enfado.*: If you don't come, I'll get mad.
  - *cómo* = "how / pardon?": *¿**Cómo**?*: Sorry, what?

- [x] **si / sí**
  - "if / whether": ***Si** llueve, me quedo.*: If it rains, I'm staying.
  - Emphatic protest: *¡**Si** ya te lo dije!*: But I already told you!
  - *sí* = "yes"
  - Emphatic *sí*: *Ella **sí** sabe cocinar.*: She really can cook.
  - Reflexive: *Habla para **sí**.*: He talks to himself.

- [x] **de**
  - Possession / "of": *El coche **de** Juan.*: Juan's car.
  - Origin: *Soy **de** Noruega.*: I'm from Norway.
  - Material / description: *Una mesa **de** madera / la chica **de** rojo.*: A wooden table / the girl in red.
  - Cause: *Morir **de** hambre.*: To be starving.
  - *de* + infinitive = "if": ***De** haberlo sabido, …*: Had I known, …

- [x] **a**
  - Direction: *Voy **a** Sevilla.*: I'm going to Seville.
  - Personal *a* (people as objects): *Veo **a** María.*: I see María.
  - Time: ***A** las tres.*: At three.
  - Manner: *Hecho **a** mano.*: Handmade.
  - *al* + infinitive = "when / on": ***Al** llegar, llamé.*: When I arrived, I called.

- [x] **mismo**
  - "same": *El **mismo** día.*: The same day.
  - "-self": *Lo hice yo **mismo**.*: I did it myself.
  - "right (now/here)": *Ahora **mismo** / aquí **mismo**.*: Right now / right here.
  - "it's all the same": *Me da lo **mismo**.*: I don't mind.

- [x] **pues**
  - Filler "well…": ***Pues**… no sé.*: Well… I don't know.
  - "then / so": *¿No te gusta? **Pues** no lo comas.*: You don't like it? Then don't eat it.
  - Emphasis: *¡**Pues** claro!*: Well, of course!
  - Formal "because": *No vino, **pues** estaba enfermo.*: He didn't come, as he was ill.

## Everyday words with many meanings (verbs and expressions)

These are richer than the small words, but less "grammar". Good for a later V3 row of tiles.

- [x] **vale**: "OK" · "it costs" (*¿Cuánto **vale**?*) · "it's worth it" (***vale** la pena*) · tag question (*…, ¿**vale**?*)
- [x] **quedar**: to meet up (***quedamos** a las ocho*) · to be left (*no **queda** pan*) · to fit/suit (*te **queda** bien*) · to agree on (***quedamos** en…*) · *quedarse* = to stay
- [x] **echar**: to throw · to pour (***echa** sal*) · to kick out · to miss (***echar** de menos*) · to burst out (***echarse** a reír*)
- [x] **dar**: to give · *me **da** igual* (I don't care) · *me **da** miedo* (it scares me) · *la ventana **da** al mar* (overlooks the sea) · ***darse** cuenta* (to realise)
- [x] **llevar**: to carry · to wear · to have been (***llevo** dos años aquí*) · to take (*¿Te **llevo**?*)
- [x] **tal**: *¿qué **tal**?* (how's it going?) · ***tal** vez* (maybe) · *con **tal** de que* (as long as) · *un **tal** Pedro* (some guy called Pedro)
- [x] **nada**: nothing · *de **nada*** (you're welcome) · ***nada** más llegar* (as soon as I arrived) · *para **nada*** (not at all)
- [x] **hecho**: done/made · a fact · *de **hecho*** (in fact) · *¡**Hecho**!* (deal!)
